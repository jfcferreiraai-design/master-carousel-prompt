# Overlay Hook

## Purpose

Overlay Hook is the active variant for informative, opinion-led, trend, and commentary carousels with a strong visual cover.

## Best Use Cases

- Trend commentary
- Industry insight
- Founder or expert takes
- Informative breakdowns
- Mini-news style posts

## Variant Promise

Slide 1 should feel like a premium editorial or news cover, then transition into fast, structured insight across the remaining slides.

## Visual Signature

- Image-led or image-like cover treatment
- Strong lower-half hook placement
- Dark fade or gradient overlay when an image is used
- Sharper contrast and stronger visual drama than a clean educational system
- Cleaner, more readable supporting slides after the cover

## Slide Sequence

Preferred default:
1. Hook cover
2. Why this matters
3. Key insight 1
4. Key insight 2
5. Key insight 3 or example
6. Takeaway
7. CTA

Six slides are acceptable if the topic needs fewer.

## Slide 1 Rules

With an image:
- Use full-bleed or near full-bleed treatment.
- Protect readability with a lower fade or overlay.
- Keep the main subject visible.
- Make the hook dominant and instantly legible.

Without an image:
- Use a strong CSS or lightweight SVG fallback.
- Build atmosphere with glow, shape, texture, or tonal layering.
- Never leave the cover blank or placeholder-like.

Slide 1 must not feel like:
- a quote card
- a tutorial opener
- a generic Canva-style text card

## Supporting Slide Rules

- Each slide should carry one dominant idea.
- Keep body copy concise and scan-friendly.
- Favor context, insight, example, takeaway, and CTA slide types.
- Avoid long feature lists, essay blocks, and heavy step-by-step systems.

## CTA Direction

- CTA should stay light by default.
- Good CTA directions: save, share, follow, comment, want more like this.
- Do not end with a hard-sell conversion push unless the user explicitly asks for it.

## Boundary

If Slide 1 stops feeling image-led and scroll-stopping, the variant is drifting away from Overlay Hook.
