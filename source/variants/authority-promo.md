# Authority Promo

## Role

This file defines the Authority Promo design layer only.

It should be applied on top of the shared Project Instructions base.
It should not redefine shared runtime behavior such as modes, preview frame rules, export rules, PT-PT rules, or HTML safety rules.

## Design Intent

Authority Promo is for offer-driven, conversion-aware, authority-building carousels for service providers, creators, consultants, and premium offers.

It should feel persuasive, polished, premium, and strategically sequenced without becoming loud or spammy.

## Cover Treatment

- Prefer a premium brand-led cover with a clear promise or positioning statement.
- Use richer gradients, polished framing, soft spotlighting, glow, or premium surfaces.
- If an image exists, portraits, mockups, screenshots, or branded visuals can support authority.
- Never let the cover feel like a cheap ad or noisy promo tile.

## Typography Direction

- Prioritize pairings that feel premium, credible, persuasive, and polished.
- Headings should feel confident and outcome-led.
- Typography can carry more drama than Clean Insight, but should stay elegant.

## Title Placement And Scale

- Place the main hook in the middle or lower-middle area with clear supporting hierarchy.
- Keep the main promise visually dominant.
- Short proof, credibility, or offer-framing lines can support the headline when useful.

## Image Expectation Behavior

- Images are optional but often useful.
- Portraits, product mockups, screenshots, and proof visuals work well here.
- Any image usage should strengthen authority, not overpower readability.

## Hierarchy

- Slides should build persuasive momentum toward the CTA.
- Promise, proof, differentiators, and next-step clarity should feel intentional.
- Visual design should signal value and credibility.

## Spacing

- Use deliberate spacing with clear emphasis zones.
- Allow stronger contrast between calm sections and high-importance proof or CTA moments.
- Keep layouts elegant and readable even when emphasis increases.

## Component Style

- Prefer proof cards, differentiator rows, credibility panels, and premium CTA treatments.
- Components should feel polished and conversion-aware.
- Decorative styling can be richer than Clean Insight, but should never look cheap.

## Slide Sequence Emphasis

- Best for promise -> why this matters -> pain -> what changes -> proof -> next step -> CTA.
- Let the sequence move clearly toward action.
- Middle slides should build trust, not just explain.

## CTA Tone

- Keep the CTA clear, premium, and deliberate.
- Good directions: book, apply, download, join, reserve, DM, enquire.
- Avoid spammy urgency or hard-sell direct-response phrasing.

## Boundary

If the carousel stops feeling premium, persuasive, and credibility-led, the output is drifting away from Authority Promo.
