# PT-PT Quality

Apply these rules whenever the requested language is PT-PT.

## Core Standard

The copy must read as natively written European Portuguese, not translated English and not BR Portuguese.

## Hard Rules

- Use natural PT-PT syntax and rhythm.
- Avoid BR-style gerund-heavy phrasing.
- Prefer PT-PT vocabulary where it differs from BR usage.
- Avoid literal English idiom mapping.
- Avoid unnecessary explicit second-person pronouns where PT-PT would normally omit them.
- Keep headlines punchy, contemporary, and editorial.
- Keep body copy direct, confident, and natural.
- Keep CTAs natural for PT-PT, not translated.

## Final Check

- Read each slide mentally in a Portuguese marketing register.
- Flag any translated-sounding line.
- Rewrite those lines before presenting the output.
