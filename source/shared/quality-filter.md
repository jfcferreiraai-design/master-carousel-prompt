# Quality Filter

Before presenting any output, verify all of the following:

- The preview is inside the full Instagram frame.
- The frame is locked to 420px width.
- Slide 1 is a real cover, not a generic intro card.
- The cover remains readable.
- Slides 2+ stay structured and focused on one dominant idea each.
- No base64 blobs or giant data URIs appear in the main HTML.
- PT-PT copy sounds native when PT-PT is requested.
- Export has not been triggered unless explicitly requested.
- The CTA feels earned, not appended.

If any check fails, fix it before presenting.
